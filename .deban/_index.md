# gaming-primitives — Index

## Brief

A research + educational website that catalogs the irreducible gameplay primitives underneath thousands of games (1958–present). Each primitive gets a researched entry (canonical originator, algorithm, variations, convergence notes) plus a tiny in-browser mini-game that demonstrates the verb. Two design goals in tension: source-readable vanilla-JS mini-games as teaching artifacts, and a nostalgic-yet-modern site that invites browsing and play.
